# CS-KMITL Announcements (`DESIGN.md`)

Inherits the root [CS-KMITL Design System](../DESIGN.md).

## Direction

- **Product**: Official department announcements, pinned notices, and broadcast statistics for CS-KMITL.
- **Character**: `editorial, calm, legible` — high-contrast typography for reading notices without visual clutter.
- **Moments**: The announcement feed with pinned priority notices and delivery/read telemetry.
- **Signature**: Hairline 1px notice cards, `tabular-nums` view counts and timestamps, and quiet category tags.
